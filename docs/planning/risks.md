# Risk register

Likelihood and impact: L (low), M (medium), H (high). Reviewed at every milestone close.

| ID   | Risk                                                                                                | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                 | Owner            | Status    |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------- |
| R-01 | Matching bug produces wrong fills (priority, partial fills, FOK) and corrupts the simulation        | M          | H      | Pure engine; property tests and a differential reference matcher on every change; replay tool to rebuild books after a fix (TB-604)                                        | Engine lead      | mitigated |
| R-02 | Non-determinism creeps into the engine (clock reads, Map iteration, random IDs) and breaks recovery | M          | H      | Engine never reads the clock; IDs and timestamps are inputs in the command; replay-equality property and integration test                                                  | Engine lead      | mitigated |
| R-03 | Journal and order table diverge after a crash between commit and in-memory install                  | L          | H      | Journal, orders, trades and settlement in one transaction; state installed only after commit; reconciliation at boot; chaos test (TB-611)                                  | Engine lead      | open      |
| R-04 | Recovery time grows with journal length and blocks restarts                                         | H          | M      | Measured ~50k commands/s replay; snapshots (TB-602) and archival (TB-609) planned for M6                                                                                   | Platform         | open      |
| R-05 | Single API instance is a single point of failure                                                    | M          | M      | Fast restart via systemd; documented backups; hot standby by journal replay (TB-608)                                                                                       | Platform         | accepted  |
| R-06 | In-process throttle counters bypassed if someone runs two instances                                 | M          | M      | Documented in deployment guide and threat model; move to Postgres (TB-813) before scale-out                                                                                | Security         | open      |
| R-07 | Out-of-band edits to cash or positions leave resting orders that cannot settle                      | L          | M      | Durable reservations with conditional UPDATE and per-account lock (TB-306, done); unfundable makers cancelled as `insufficient_funds`; reconciliation checks reserved cash | Engine lead      | mitigated |

| R-11 | Users mistake the simulator for a real trading service                                              | L          | H      | "Simulated venue · paper trading only" in UI chrome, README and API description; fictional instruments; no external connectivity                                           | Product          | mitigated |
| R-12 | Market-data fan-out saturates the event loop with many subscribers                                  | M          | M      | Slow-consumer disconnect today; conflation and send budgets (TB-407); fan-out benchmark (TB-409)                                                                           | Platform         | open      |
